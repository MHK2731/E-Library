About Us

DPS Jankipuram

Students of DPS Jankipuram, through their club The Cybernauts, aim to promote reading amidst declining habits due to technology. They are launching an E-Library, BOOKPORT, which features only open access e-books contributed by members of the club. Initially stocked with a few books, new titles will be added weekly, providing a seamless reading experience on digital devices. Explore BOOKPORT and discover your next read!
